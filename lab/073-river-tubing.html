How to float

← → / A D
Kick left or right across the current.
↑ ↓
Paddle ahead, or back-paddle to slow down.
Space
Duck under a low branch (brief, with a short cooldown).

Pause.
Touch
Drag anywhere on the river: your tube kicks toward your finger.

What's in the water

  • Limestone rocks: a hit costs a tube
  • Cypress knees near the banks
  • Low branches: duck under for +15
  • Lost flip-flops: +25 each
  • Cooler buddy: absorbs one hit
  • Rapids speed you up; clear one for +40
  • Eddies spin you and stall you

The Lazy Oxbow is fictional. On real rivers: wear a life jacket, check the flow before you go, and pack out everything you bring in.
