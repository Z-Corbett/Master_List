          Dot product rewards long vectors: cake and pizza were given big magnitudes (think "very frequent word"), so they climb the dot-product list for any food. Euclidean distance punishes length differences. Tick Unit-normalise and all three agree, because for unit vectors a · b = cos θ and ‖a − b‖² = 2 − 2 cos θ.

          Honest caveats
          • These vectors were built by hand with named axes, so the tidy answers are partly by construction. Real embeddings have hundreds of unnamed dimensions.
          • The famous word2vec result leaves the input words out of the answers. Allow them and the nearest vector is often just A or C. Untick the box above to see the full ranking.
          • It always returns some word. palace − king + village gets a confident-looking answer to a question that means nothing.
          • In real models analogies are unreliable, and they echo whatever the training text says, stereotypes included.
