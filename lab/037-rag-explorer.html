RAG Pipeline Explorer

Retrieval-augmented generation, slowed down so you can watch it. A handbook is cut into chunks, each chunk becomes a vector, your question becomes a vector, and the closest chunks are pasted into the prompt. Change the knobs and see what the model would — and wouldn't — get to read.

Toy model“Embeddings” here are 256-dimension hashed bag-of-words with TF-IDF weights, computed in your browser. No real model, no network — similar in shape to a real pipeline, not in quality.

      Trade-offs to try
      
    
      
      
      Small chunks
      Precise matches and cheap to include, but an answer longer than a chunk can never arrive whole. Try the shift-swap question at 15 words.
      
      
      Big chunks
      Answers stay whole, but each vector averages many topics, scores flatten, and a few chunks eat the whole context budget.
      
      
      Overlap
      Repeating words across boundaries rescues split answers at the cost of duplicated text in the index and the prompt. Try the lens question at 200 words with 0 overlap, then 20.
      
      
      Words vs meaning
      This toy only matches words. “Pets” never meets “dogs”. Real embedding models capture much of that — but still miss things, so measure retrieval separately from generation.
