Wind Chimes

A ring of tubes on the back porch, a striker on a string and a sail to catch the breeze. Gusts come from seeded noise, the striker swings as a pendulum, and every collision rings a stack of synthesized partials. Tap a tube to ring it yourself.

Tubes use the mode ratios of a free–free bar (1 : 2.756 : 5.404 : 8.933). The bell set uses the classic church-bell partials (hum, prime, minor-third tierce, quint and nominal at 0.5 : 1 : 1.2 : 1.5 : 2) on the four notes of the Westminster Quarters plus the octave. Keys 1–7 ring tubes.
