    Volunteer mechanics

    You don't need to be a pro. New volunteers shadow a lead mechanic for their first two shifts, and we teach the rest: patching tubes, adjusting brakes, indexing gears, truing wheels. Pros, we need you to teach.

    Earn-a-bike

    Build it, ride it home

    No car, no bike, no problem. Put in shop hours and you'll leave on a bike you rebuilt yourself, with a lock, lights and a helmet. Ages 12 and up; under-16s come with a grown-up.

    1. OrientationA one-hour safety walk-through at any open shop. Learn where the tools live.
    2. Put in 12 hoursHelp around the shop: sorting parts, stripping donated bikes, sweeping up. Hours are logged on your card.
    3. Pick a frameChoose from the donated bikes that fit you, with a mechanic checking the size.
    4. Rebuild & rideOverhaul it with a mentor, pass a safety check, and it's yours, with lock, lights and helmet.
