Threads

    Each colour is matched to the nearest of 20 generic thread colours by CIEDE2000 colour difference (ΔE00). Names only, no brand numbers: hold real skeins against the fabric before you buy.

    Suggested stitches

    ShapeThreadAcrossStitch

    BackstitchFor outlines, stems and anything thinner than about 2 mm (0.08 in). Each stitch goes back to meet the end of the last, so the line is unbroken.
    Satin stitchFor small shapes up to about ⅜ in across. Straight stitches laid side by side cover the shape; longer ones snag and sag, which is why wider shapes get the next stitch.
    Long-and-shortFor shapes wider than ⅜ in. A first row of alternating long and short stitches, then rows that split into them, so no stitch is long and colours can blend.

    Transfer pattern

    Print at 100% ("Actual size"), not "Fit to page", then measure the square: it should be exactly 1 inch.

    Calibration: this square is 1 in × 1 in when printed at 100%. If it isn't, the pattern is off by the same factor.
