        Good to know

        Questions

        Where do I park?

        In the school lot and the hayfield across County Road 118, with a golf-cart shuttle every ten minutes. Accessible parking is behind the parish hall; enter from Mesquite Street.

        Do I have to be present to win the raffle?

        No. Winners are called and posted on the parish bulletin board. Tickets are sold to adults 18 and over.

        Can I pay with a card?

        Yes at the scrip tents and the raffle table. Individual booths only take festival scrip, which keeps the lines moving.

        Is the festival pet friendly?

        Leashed, friendly dogs are welcome on the grounds, but not inside the parish hall or the kids' corner.

        What is the blessing of the gardens?

        St. Fiacre is the patron saint of gardeners, so on Saturday afternoon Father blesses seeds, seedlings and garden tools brought by parishioners, followed by a seed swap.

        Where does the money go?

        To the parish school's tuition-assistance fund and the roof over the parish hall, which has been patient long enough.
