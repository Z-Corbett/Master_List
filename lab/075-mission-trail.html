The five missions

Founded by Franciscan missionaries in the 18th century, the missions were communities where Indigenous people, many of them Coahuiltecan, lived, farmed and worshipped. Their acequias, the irrigation ditches that watered the mission fields, are part of what the World Heritage listing recognises.

    Who looks after what

    Concepción, San José, San Juan and Espada make up San Antonio Missions National Historical Park. The Alamo, which was Mission San Antonio de Valero, is managed separately by the State of Texas.

    Living churches

    The four southern mission churches are still active Catholic parishes, so you may arrive during a Mass, a wedding or a funeral. Visit quietly, and check with the parish before planning around a service.
