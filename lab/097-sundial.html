Sundial

Give it a place on Earth and it lays out a horizontal sundial for that latitude, sets the gnomon, and casts today's shadow from the sun's actual position. Then it corrects the dial's reading for the equation of time and your longitude, so you can tell how far the dial is from your watch.

The figure eight is the analemma: plotted against the equation of time (x) and the sun's declination (y), it's where the sun stands at the same clock time every day. The dial runs fast when the curve is right of centre and slow when it's left.
