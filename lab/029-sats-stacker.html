Why DCA feels better

Buying on a schedule spreads your entries across highs and lows, so a crash early on hurts less and even buys you cheaper sats.

Why lump sum often wins

In a market that trends up, money invested earlier has longer to grow. Flip the trend to Bear and watch that reverse.

What to look at

Compare "worst vs cost" and "days underwater": they're a better guide to how a strategy feels to live through than the final number.
