What people see

For decades, visitors on the high desert east of Marfa have reported glowing orbs low over Mitchell Flat, toward the Chinati Mountains: lights that hover, drift, split apart, merge and fade.

There is a roadside viewing area on US 90 east of town where people gather after dark to watch for them.

What might explain them

Investigators have attributed many sightings to distant vehicle headlights and to mirages, where layers of warm and cool air bend light across the flat. Not everyone is convinced, and the mystery is part of the draw.

The lights here are pure invention: seeded noise decides where each one drifts, when it splits and when two merge.

The sky overhead

This scene faces north, so the stars wheel counterclockwise around Polaris, about 15° per hour. Marfa sits near 30° north, so Polaris stands about 30° above the horizon.
