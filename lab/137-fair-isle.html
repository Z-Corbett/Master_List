Fair Isle Chart

A seeded generator for Fair Isle bands: little peerie patterns, big OXO and star bands, and scattered seeding stitches. It keeps the Fair Isle rule of never more than two colours in a round, and every motif repeat divides your stitch count so the pattern closes cleanly at the seam.

      Floats

        A float is the strand of the colour you aren't using, carried across the back. Long floats snag fingers, so knitters catch them down ("trap" them) every few stitches. Knitting in the round, the last stitch of a round sits next to the first, so a run that crosses the seam counts as one float here.

        Chart

        Read the chart from the bottom up and from right to left: row 1 is the bottom row, and stitch 1 is on the right. That's the direction your needle travels when you knit in the round, so every round starts at the right-hand edge. Row numbers sit on the right; red outlines mark floats that are too long.

        Knitted preview

        Each stitch is drawn as the V a knit stitch makes on the right side, a little wider than tall, as at a typical stranded gauge of 28 stitches and 32 rounds to 10 cm.
