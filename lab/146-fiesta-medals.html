Fiesta Medal Maker

Fiesta is San Antonio's big spring celebration, and one of its best-loved customs is the Fiesta medal: a pin on a bright ribbon, made by schools, clubs, businesses and neighbours. People wear dozens of them on sashes, hats and vests, and swap them with friends and strangers. Design your own here, pin it to your sash, and try your luck with a vendor.
