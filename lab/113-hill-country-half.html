Prices go up as race day gets closer. All times are US Central (CST). Online registration closes at the end of Thursday, March 4.

The course

One loop for the half, an out-and-back for the 10K, both from the ranch's hay barn. Expect rocky climbs to the Lookout, a shaded descent, and ankle-deep water at Cedar Creek.
