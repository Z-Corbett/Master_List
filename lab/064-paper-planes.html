A simplified flight model. Lift and drag grow with the square of airspeed and follow a basic lift curve that stalls past about 14–18° angle of attack. Gravity pulls the plane down, and the plane pitches toward a trim angle set by fold style and nose weight. It is tuned to feel like a paper plane, not to predict one. Wind is a seeded breeze with gusts.
