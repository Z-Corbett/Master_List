    Pit notes

      A game model, not a recipe: the heat, the stall (evaporative cooling of the surface) and the wrap are simplified so a 12-hour cook fits in a few minutes. The "done" window of roughly 200–205°F internal is a common pitmaster rule of thumb; real brisket is done when a probe slides in with little resistance.

      Food safety note: keep resting meat at or above 140°F until you serve it.
