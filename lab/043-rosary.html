Mysteries

Which mysteries to pray

This is a common custom, not a rule. The usual weekly pattern gives the Glorious Mysteries to every Sunday. The Luminous Mysteries were added by St. John Paul II in 2002 (Rosarium Virginis Mariae).

Prayer texts are the traditional English versions, which are in the public domain. The mysteries and weekly schedule follow common Catholic practice.
