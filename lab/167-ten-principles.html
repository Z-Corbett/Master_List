Ten Principles

In the late 1970s the industrial designer Dieter Rams set down ten principles of good design. They are usually quoted as ten short sentences, and they are easy to nod along to. This tour tries them instead: each one is a small experiment on an invented product, the Pell 3 kitchen timer (fictional), with a number you can watch change.

Move with the list, the buttons, or ← → Home End. Every principle has its own link.

    The ten principle headings are Dieter Rams's, as widely cited. Every explanation, demo and number on this page is original, and the Pell 3 timer is invented; no real product or brand is shown. The small models behind the numbers (search time, gauge scaling, style half-lives, energy use) are illustrative simplifications, stated where they are used.
