Glaze Mixer

Enter a recipe as percentages of raw materials, add colorants on top, scale it to a batch, and see its Unity Molecular Formula (the Seger formula): moles of each oxide with the fluxes set to 1.0, built from published material analyses and molecular weights computed from IUPAC atomic weights.

Raw glaze materials can be toxic

  • Wear a properly fitted respirator (e.g. N95 or P100) when handling dry materials; silica dust causes silicosis. Clean up wet, never by sweeping.
  • Barium, lead and some colorants (cobalt, copper, manganese, chrome and others) are hazardous. This tool has no lead or barium materials, on purpose.
  • The UMF can't tell you a fired glaze is food-safe. Only a lab leach test of the fired ware can.
  • Fire test tiles in your own kiln before any production batch: materials, application and firing all change the result.

    Moles of each oxide = Σ (material % × oxide % ÷ 100) ÷ oxide molecular weight, then everything is divided by the total moles of flux oxides. Colorant additions are left out of the UMF (a common convention); loss on ignition (CO₂, water) burns off and never counts. B₂O₃ is listed with the stabilisers by convention, though it acts as a flux. A thermal-expansion estimate is deliberately not shown: published coefficient sets disagree, and I won't present one without a source I can check.

    Material analyses used

    Oxide weight % (fired, LOI excluded). Real materials vary from lot to lot.
    1. Theoretical compounds (silica SiO₂, whiting CaCO₃, talc Mg₃Si₄O₁₀(OH)₂, wollastonite CaSiO₃, zinc oxide ZnO) are computed here from their formulas and atomic weights. Digitalfire lists the same theoretical talc (MgO 31.87, SiO₂ 63.38) and wollastonite (CaO 48.28, SiO₂ 51.72). Real talc and wollastonite always carry impurities.
    2. EPK kaolin: Digitalfire EP Kaolin, the Feldspar Corp. 2001 analysis.
    3. Custer feldspar: Digitalfire Custer Feldspar (pre-2000). Later lots were reported lower in K₂O, and Digitalfire reports the mine stopped supplying it in 2024. Treat it as a historical analysis.
    4. Nepheline syenite: Digitalfire generic analysis, consistent with its A270 grade page (within the listed tolerances).
    5. Gerstley borate: Digitalfire Gerstley Borate, matching New Mexico Clay's typical analysis to 0.1%. A natural, variable material.
    6. Ferro frit 3134: Digitalfire Ferro Frit 3134 (includes 2% Al₂O₃). The older analysis on Ceramics Today's frit chart (SiO₂ 46.5, CaO 20.1, B₂O₃ 23.1, Na₂O 10.3) has no alumina.
    7. Atomic weights: IUPAC abridged standard atomic weights (2021).
