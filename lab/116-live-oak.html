Live Oak

A few rewriting rules, applied over and over with a seeded coin toss, grow a string of symbols; a turtle walks the string and draws. Tuned the right way, it comes out low, wide and sprawling like a Texas live oak, with Spanish moss hanging off the long limbs.

Axiom & rules

F, G draw forward · f moves without drawing · + − turn left / right · [ ] save / restore the turtle · | turns round · L hangs a leaf cluster · any other letter just rewrites.

The same rules at a small iteration count, walked one symbol at a time with no jitter or sprawl, so every segment lines up exactly with the symbol that drew it. Click a segment to find its symbol.

  • + −: turn
