Accessible Data Table

A sortable, filterable, paginated table with selectable rows that follows the ARIA grid pattern: one tab stop, arrow-key navigation between cells, aria-sort on the sorted column, row and column indices that survive pagination, and a live region that says what changed. The panel on the right shows the attributes a screen reader gets for whichever cell has focus.
