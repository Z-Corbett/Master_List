Example Mapping workshop board

Before writing a line of Gherkin, map the story: the rules it has to follow, concrete examples of each rule, and the questions nobody can answer yet. A technique introduced by Matt Wynne of the Cucumber team; this board is a practice space with a made-up shop.

How the board works

  1. Write the story on the yellow card.
  2. Each blue card is one rule (an acceptance criterion). Under it, green cards are concrete examples — “the one where the card has expired”.
  3. Red cards park questions so the conversation keeps moving.
  4. Drag a green card by its ⠿ grip to another rule. From the keyboard, focus the grip and use ← → to move it between rules and ↑ ↓ to reorder.
  5. Lots of red means the story isn't ready; lots of blue means it's probably two stories.
  6. Write Given / When / Then on separate lines of a green card and the export keeps them.
