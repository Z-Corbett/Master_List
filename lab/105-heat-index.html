Heat Index

How hot it feels when humidity stops sweat from cooling you. This page runs the algorithm the NWS Weather Prediction Center publishes: first a simple estimate, then, if that reaches 80 °F, the full Rothfusz regression with its two humidity adjustments. The map shows the whole temperature × humidity plane in the NWS risk bands.
