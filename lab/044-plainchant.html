How it is composed

Each verse rises from the final to the reciting tone (the tenor), recites there, bends at the mid-verse pause, then falls back by step to close on the final. Almost every move is a step or a repeated note, as in real chant. The notes stay within the mode's range, and the drone always sounds the final.

The sound

A sawtooth voice shaped by three formant filters for each Latin vowel, a soft drone an octave below the final, and a synthetic five-second reverb standing in for a stone nave. Texts are from Psalm 150 and the Gloria Patri, with the Douay-Rheims English.
