A few true things

Why the roadsides turn blue

Planted in autumn

Texas bluebonnets are winter annuals: seeds sprout in fall, sit out the cold as flat leafy rosettes, then bolt and bloom when spring warms up.

A state flower, plural

The bluebonnet became the Texas state flower in 1901, and in 1971 the designation was widened to cover every native Texas lupine.

No picking law, but manners

Picking one isn't a crime, contrary to the popular myth. Trespassing is, and trampled patches set fewer seeds for next year. Photograph from the edge.
