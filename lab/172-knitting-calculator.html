Units

1 · Gauge

In stockinette (or the pattern's stitch), after blocking.

Or measure a swatch: count stitches and rows across part of it.

2 · Cast on

For a target width, rounded to the nearest count that fits the stitch pattern.

3 · Increase or decrease evenly

The classic "increase 8 sts evenly across the row", written out stitch by stitch.

k = knit, M1 = make one (lift the bar between stitches and knit into its back), k2tog = knit two together. [ ] n times = work the bracket n times in all. Flat rows keep shaping away from the edge stitches.

4 · Yardage (rough)

A model, not a promise: stitch pattern, ease and size swing it a lot.

Model: area × rows per inch × 5.5 in of yarn per inch of row, shown as a rough range from 0.8× to 1.25× and rounded to 10. Typical rows are stitches × 1.4. Buy an extra skein from the same dye lot.

5 · Convert a pattern to your gauge

Same finished size, your stitches. Uses your gauge from section 1.

Stitch counts are rounded to the repeat set in section 2.
