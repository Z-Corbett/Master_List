        Channels

        Simplified model. This is a teaching simulation, not the Lightning protocol. It has no HTLCs, timelocks, onion encryption or channel reserves, and nothing is on-chain. Fees are a base fee plus a proportional rate in parts per million, rounded up to whole sats (real nodes use millisatoshis). The sender knows every channel's capacity and fee policy but not its balance split, just as on the real network, and learns about a short hop only when a payment fails there. At most five attempts are made. All nodes are fictional.
