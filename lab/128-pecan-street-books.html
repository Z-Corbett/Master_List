        Sell us your books

        Add the books you're thinking of bringing in and get a rough offer. It is only an estimate: the buyer at the counter has the last word.

        How we price an offer

        1. We start from the printed cover price and pay a share of it that depends on condition:
          ConditionShare of cover price
        2. Hardcovers add $0.50 each before rounding.
        3. Each book's cash offer is rounded down to the nearest 25¢, and any book we accept is worth at least 25¢.
        4. Damaged books (water, mould, loose pages) get no offer, but we'll happily recycle them.
        5. Store credit is 150% of the cash total, rounded down to the cent.
        6. Up to 20 books per visit.
