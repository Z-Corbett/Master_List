Constrained Decoding

A language model only ever picks the next token. To make it produce valid JSON, check every token in the vocabulary against a grammar before sampling. Tokens that would break the grammar get probability zero, and what's left is renormalised. The "model" here is a toy with a 26-token vocabulary and seeded fake logits. It has mostly learned the format but has a few bad habits.
