The protocol maximum is shown. Actual spendable supply is a little lower: the genesis block's 50 BTC can't be spent, and some early miners claimed less than the full subsidy.

Built by Zac, a long-time Bitcoin enthusiast, with BigInt arithmetic so no floating point touches a satoshi. No network calls: the current height is estimated from block 840,000 (20 April 2024) at ten minutes per block.
