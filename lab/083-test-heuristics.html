Test Heuristics Deck

Two heuristics that help you think of tests you'd otherwise miss. SFDIPOT walks the parts of a product you could test — it's the product-elements list from James Bach's Heuristic Test Strategy Model. HICCUPPS(F) lists ways to recognise a problem when you see one, by inconsistency with something you trust — the consistency oracles described by James Bach and Michael Bolton, often extended with an F. The summaries below are this page's own paraphrases.

Feature under test (fictional): “Split the bill” in the Tabletop dining app.
  • Split a restaurant bill among 2–12 diners: evenly, by item, or with custom amounts.
  • Add a tip as a percentage; totals are rounded to the cent and any leftover cent goes to the organiser.
  • Each diner gets a pay link; the bill locks 24 hours after it is created.
