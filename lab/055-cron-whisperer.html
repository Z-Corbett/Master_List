Cron Whisperer

Type a cron expression or build it field by field. It reads the schedule back in plain English, lists the next ten runs in any IANA timezone — and tells you exactly what happens when daylight saving skips or repeats an hour.

    Field reference

    FieldValuesNotes
    second (optional)0–59Only when the expression has six fields.
    minute0–59* any · 5 · 1,15,30 list · 0-29 range · */15 or 10-50/20 step
    hour0–23Local time in the chosen timezone.
    day of month1–31Days that don't exist in a month (31 April) never match.
    month1–12 or JAN–DECNames are case-insensitive.
    day of week0–7 or SUN–SAT0 and 7 are both Sunday.

    The OR rule: when both day-of-month and day-of-week are restricted, a day matches if either matches — 0 0 1 * MON runs on the 1st and on every Monday. A field counts as unrestricted only if it starts with * (so, as in Vixie cron, */2 in one day field means the other is ANDed). Macros: @yearly, @monthly, @weekly, @daily, @hourly.
