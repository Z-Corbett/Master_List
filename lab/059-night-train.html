    Before you board

    Questions, answered

    When should I arrive at Harrowgate Central?

    Boarding opens at 19:30 on Platform 9. Your attendant will meet you at your car; there is no security queue, so arriving twenty minutes before departure is plenty.

    Can I board or leave at an intermediate station?

    Yes. Fares are priced for the whole berth, whichever stations you use. Late-night stops at Coldwater Junction and Ashby Ridge are served by a quiet boarding door so sleepers aren't woken.

    What happens to my hold?

    Held berths are kept for exactly ten minutes. After that they return to the pool automatically. You can release them sooner with one tap.

    Is there somewhere to store bicycles and large luggage?

    The baggage car at the front of the train takes bicycles, skis and trunks. Bring an overnight bag to your cabin; everything else can be checked through to Port Aurelia.

    Is The Night Owl a real train?

    No. The Night Owl, its stations and its fares are invented for this page, a design study of what booking a sleeper train could feel like.
