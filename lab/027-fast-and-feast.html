Fast &
Feast

A Lenten meal planner for the whole household. Put in everyone's age, and it works out who fasts and who abstains on each day of this year's Lent. Then plan a week of meatless meals and take the shopping list with you.

A planning aid, not a rule book. This follows the general US norms: abstinence from meat on Ash Wednesday and the Fridays of Lent from age 14; fasting on Ash Wednesday and Good Friday from 18 to 59. Illness, pregnancy and other circumstances excuse, and bishops can dispense. Ask your pastor or diocese about your situation.

1 · Your household ages decide the obligations

Fasting means one full meal and two smaller meals that together don't equal a full one, with nothing in between. Abstinence means no meat; fish, eggs and dairy are fine.
