Prairie Pinball

Sunset over the pasture, a windmill turning in the lane, and bluebonnets that pop the ball right back at you. Light S-T-A-R for a bigger multiplier, take the Cattle Drive ramp, and don't shake the cabinet too hard.

How to play

Z / ←
Left flipper. On touch screens, press the left half of the table.
M / →
Right flipper, or the right half of the table.
Space / ↓
Hold to pull the plunger back, and release to launch. A longer pull launches harder.
N
Nudge the cabinet. Two quick nudges bring a warning; a third tilts the table.
Ball save
For 8 seconds after each launch, a drained ball comes back.
S-T-A-R
Roll through all four top lanes to raise the multiplier (up to 5×). Flippers shift the lit lanes.
Scoring
Bluebonnet bumpers 100, slingshots 10, lanes 50, each windmill turn 25, Cattle Drive ramp 1,000. Every score is multiplied.

Press Start, then hold the plunger.
