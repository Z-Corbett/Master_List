Swing the loop over your head, aim, and throw. The rope is simulated link by link, so it flies, trails and settles like a rope. You score a calf only if the loop lands around its head, so lead your throw where the calf is going.

Scoring

  • A catch scores 100 (easy), 150 (medium) or 200 (hard), plus 1 point for every 10 px the calf stood from you.
  • Three catches in a row or more and each one scores double.
  • A miss ends the streak. The clock runs 60 seconds.
  • Swing longer for a stronger throw: full power after 1.5 s.

How the rope works

The rope is 30 points joined by fixed-length links, integrated with Verlet steps at 120 per second and relaxed 16 times per step. The loop's links close into a ring held open like a stiff honda loop. A catch is judged with a point-in-polygon test: the calf's head must be inside the ring when it lands.
