What the colour numbers mean (and don't)

The page averages a 7 × 7 pixel patch where you tap and converts it from sRGB to CIELAB (D65). The Individual Typology Angle is ITA = arctan((L* − 50) / b*) in degrees, a measure from dermatology research (Chardon et al., 1991) that describes how light a colour is relative to its yellowness. It is a description, not a ranking. The undertone is a rough guess from the balance of b* (yellow) to a* (red): a hue angle h = atan2(b*, a*) of 60° or more reads warm, 50° or less reads cool, and in between reads neutral.

It is only a colour heuristic. There is no recognition of hands or skin here, just the pixels you tap. Warm bulbs, shade, screens and camera white balance all shift the numbers, so try a sample in daylight, and trust your own eye over the maths.
