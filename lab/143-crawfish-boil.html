Crawfish Boil Planner

Houston's Vietnamese-American cooks helped popularise a crawfish style often called Viet-Cajun: a Louisiana-style boil, then a toss in garlic butter with lemongrass and Cajun spice. This planner scales a boil for your crowd. Every quantity is a planning estimate, not a recipe from any restaurant, so taste as you go.

The crowd

Appetite
Live crawfish per person. A common rule of thumb is 3–5 lb each; it's a rule of thumb, not a law.
Spice level
Spice scales the boil seasoning, the chili in the butter and the soak time.
In the pot too
Food safety: only cook crawfish that are alive when they go in the pot. Rinse them in clean water, then pick out and discard any dead ones (limp, not moving) before cooking. Keep them cool and ventilated, not submerged, until the boil.
