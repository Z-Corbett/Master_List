Terrarium

A sealed jar with its own weather. Light warms the soil and leaves, water rises as vapour, beads up on the cool glass at night and runs back down. Ferns unfurl, fittonia spreads its veined leaves, the moss thickens, and a colony of springtails (tiny soil animals that live on mould and decay) keeps the place clean.
