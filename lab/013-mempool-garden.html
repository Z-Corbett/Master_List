    No blocks yet. Wait for the countdown, or press “Mine block now”.

    How it works: the block template is built greedily — sort pending transactions by fee rate (sat per virtual byte), then take each one that still fits under the scaled block limit of 40,000 vB. Real miners also account for parent/child packages; this toy treats every transaction as independent. When the pool passes 3,000 transactions the cheapest are purged, just like a node's mempool minimum fee rising.
