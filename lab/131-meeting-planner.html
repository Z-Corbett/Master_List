    How it works

    Offsets are never typed in. For each half-hour the page asks Intl.DateTimeFormat with a timeZone what the wall clock reads there, which draws on the browser's copy of the IANA time zone database, daylight saving rules included.

    The US moves its clocks on the second Sunday in March and the first Sunday in November; the EU on the last Sundays of March and October. For two or three weeks each spring and one each autumn the gap between New York and London is 4 hours instead of 5.

    India stays at UTC+5:30 all year. Lord Howe Island, off New South Wales, runs at UTC+10:30 and moves only half an hour in summer, to UTC+11.
