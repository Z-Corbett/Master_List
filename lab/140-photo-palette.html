Photo Palette

Pull a crafting palette out of any photo: the colours, how much of the picture each one covers, what to call them, which pairs are readable as text, and what they'd look like as yarn, fabric or polish.

Your picture

Your photo stays on your device. It's read with the browser's File API and drawn into a canvas on this page; nothing is uploaded, sent or stored.

Extraction

Method

Palette

    Names come from a built-in list of 40 colours; each swatch takes the nearest by ΔE*ab (CIE 1976: straight-line distance in CIELAB, D65 white). Under about 2.3 is roughly a just-noticeable difference.

    Contrast

    Row = text colour, column = background. WCAG 2 contrast ratio, (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05) from relative luminance. AA needs 4.5 for normal text, AA large 3 for large text; pass/fail uses the unrounded ratio.
