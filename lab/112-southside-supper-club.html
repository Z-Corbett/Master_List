      A note from the hosts

      Why a long table

      Our grandmother fed whoever showed up on Sunday, and nobody ever asked who invited them. That's the whole idea here. We cook what's in season from growers within a couple of hours of town, put it on one table, and seat you next to someone you haven't met.

      Come as you are. Bring an appetite and a story. We'll handle the cornbread.

      — Delphine & Otis Marchbanks, your (invented) hosts
