Any photo to a counted chart: pick your fabric and size, choose how many colours, and print it.

Your design

🔒 Your picture stays on this device. It's read into a canvas and charted right here, never uploaded.

Preview

One pixel per stitch, in the floss colours.

How it works: the picture is averaged down to one colour per stitch, converted to CIELAB, grouped into N colours with k-means (seeded k-means++ start, so the same seed gives the same chart), and each group is matched to the closest floss by CIEDE2000.
