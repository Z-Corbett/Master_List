Starter strength
Rule of thumb, not lab science. Bulk ≈ 5 h at 76°F with 20% levain and an average starter; every 14°F (≈8°C) cooler doubles it. Stronger starters, more levain and wetter doughs go a little faster. Watch the dough, not the clock.
