The assistant

Parcelwise Assist answers customers and can call tools:
  • lookup_order — the signed-in customer's orders
  • issue_refund — up to 40 credits
  • redirect_parcel — change a delivery address
  • send_email — reply to the customer
  • fetch_page — read a web page or email
Safe — ordinary request; help normally.
Prompt injection — text trying to override the assistant's instructions, often hidden in content it reads.
Data exfiltration — trying to get data out that the asker shouldn't have.
Jailbreak — role-play or framing to talk the model out of its rules.
