Following the edgesTiles are grown like superpixels: each pixel joins the nearby tile it is closest to in both position and colour, measured in a square rotated to the local edge direction (from a smoothed image gradient).
Grout and bevelA distance transform from the tile boundaries lays the grout line and gives each tessera a slightly lifted, light-catching rim.
Gold leafByzantine gold tesserae were gold leaf fused between layers of glass and set at uneven angles so they catch the light. Here each gilded tile gets its own tilt.
