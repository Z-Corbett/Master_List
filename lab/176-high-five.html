The five levels

  1. Frontage road, at grade
  2. The main lanes
  3. First connector ramp
  4. Second connector ramp
  5. The top connector, about 120 ft up

The real High Five, where I-635 crosses US-75, opened in 2005 and stacks five levels of roadway. This game borrows its shape and its reputation; the signs and the route are made up.
