Coloring Pages.

Generate, color, print. A fresh page for every seed.

Everything is drawn in your browser: generated line art, or a photo traced with Sobel edge detection. Nothing is uploaded or fetched.

Make a page

🔒 Your photo stays on this device. It's read into a canvas and processed right here, never uploaded.

Line weight

How it works: greyscale, a small Gaussian blur, the Sobel operator for edge strength, a threshold, then specks under the size limit are removed and lines optionally thickened by one pixel. There's no AI here, so busy photos make busy pages; simple, high-contrast pictures work best.

Color it in

Pick a crayon and tap any closed shape to fill it. Keyboard: focus the page, move the ring with the arrow keys (Shift for bigger steps) and press Enter to fill. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes. Printing prints only the page.
