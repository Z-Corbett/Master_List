Polyrhythm
Metronome

A click with accents and subdivisions, or two voices at once: m beats against n in every cycle. The dial draws each voice as a polygon, and the hand sweeps once per cycle, so you can see where they meet.

Mode
Transport
Tap tempo

Tap four times, or press T. A pause of 2 s starts a new count.

Voices
Presets

Tempo counts voice B, the ground beat: one cycle lasts n beats, and voice A fits m evenly spaced beats into the same span.

Accents (press a beat to toggle)

Stopped. Sound starts when you press Play.
