Landings

    To dock, bring the barge alongside the wooden landing and slow to under 6 on the speed readout (the meter turns gold). Each landing is worth 50, plus 10 per passenger.

    How the barge handles

    The engine pushes against water drag, so speed builds and fades gradually. The rudder doesn't turn the boat directly; it spins it up, and the turn rate is capped at about 34° per second. It bites harder the faster you're moving, so a barge at rest barely turns.

    Each fresh knock on the walls costs 15 points. Reaching the turning basin at the top ends the tour, with a point for every second left of the five-minute run.
