Equal vs just

Twelve-tone equal temperament splits the octave into twelve identical steps of 21/12. Just intonation tunes intervals to small whole-number ratios, so their harmonics line up exactly. Pick a root and an interval or chord to see how far apart the two systems are, in hertz, in cents and in beats per second.
